Smart Coordinate Search & Grid

Parity Overview

Z EARTH vs. Google Earth Pro

How Z EARTH delivers an open, independent desktop GIS experience.

Feature / Requirement Z EARTH Google Earth Pro
Open Source & Extensible Codebase ✓ Yes (.NET 10 & CesiumJS) ✗ No (Proprietary Closed Source)
User Privacy & Local Storage ✓ 100% Local (No Account Required) ✗ Telemetry & Account Bound
Bilingual Arabic & English UI ✓ Complete Native Support (AR/EN) ✗ Limited RTL / Arabic UX
KML / KMZ Places & Hierarchy ✓ Complete Round-trip Support ✓ Supported
Automatic UTM & Metric Coordinates ✓ Smart Zone Calculation Partial (Manual config)
NASA Daily Satellite Observations ✓ NASA GIBS MODIS Layers ✗ No Direct GIBS Feed
